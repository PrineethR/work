About

Design, play, tech, humans
Bengaluru, India

I combine a background in electrical engineering with play experience design, and try to build the world in a not-so-boring way. The transdisciplinary route looks unconventional on paper; in practice it is what lets me move through complex problems while keeping the person at the centre of them.

My practice moves between extremes — zooming out to strategy, then down into detail. I work well in the rapid prototype and in the slow refinement, in the cross-functional workshop and in focused execution. Moving between those scales is where I notice connections that are otherwise easy to miss.

Beyond making interfaces that look good, I hold that design can reshape our relationship with technology, with each other and with the world. Every project, however small, carries that potential — to leave things a little better and a little more human than I found them.
